Privacy Policy — Mistri Hazir Ho

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

Mistri Hazir Ho is a professional service directory app that connects customers with skilled workers across Pakistan. The app is operated by Muhammad Tehseen Javed.

For all inquiries — general or privacy-related — contact us at: snaptransteam@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect — And What We Do Not

We built this app on a single, non-negotiable principle: collect nothing that is not essential, and make it technically impossible to collect what we do not need.

Customer Accounts — Zero Personal Data. By Design.

When a customer registers, the app asks for only two things:

  • A username of their own choosing — a nickname, a made-up name, anything they like (e.g. "ironman1", "parrotman"). A real name is never asked for, never required, and there is no field in the app where a real name could even be entered.
  • A password — encrypted and never readable by us.

That is all. There is no field for a phone number. No field for an email address. No field for a photo. No "Sign in with Google" or any social login that would link us to an account holding personal data. A customer is, by the design of this app, completely anonymous. We did not just choose not to collect personal data — we removed every option through which a customer could even accidentally provide it.

Worker / Professional Accounts — Voluntary Public Listings

Workers create a professional public listing — identical in nature to what a worker or business already displays on a signboard, a visiting card, a pamphlet, or a shopfront banner. Workers choose what they share. This may include:

  • A display name — a professional nickname, trade name, or business name. There is no mechanism in the app to verify or identify a real name. Whatever the worker chooses to write is their choice entirely.
  • A phone number — workers and businesses have always displayed a contact number publicly on their signs and cards. This is no different. It is the worker's professional contact number, shared by their own decision, so customers can reach them.
  • A profile photo — optional. Many professionals use their image on banners and storefronts so the public can recognise them. Workers who prefer not to share a photo can skip this entirely.
  • City and skill category — selected from a list. No maps, no coordinates, no live tracking of any kind.
  • An optional CCTV workspace URL — entirely voluntary. A clinic may add a live waiting-room feed so elderly or mobility-limited patients can check how busy it is before travelling. A workshop may add it so customers can see work in progress. This is a business convenience tool, activated only if the professional chooses it.

This information is shared voluntarily and publicly, exactly as any business shares its details on a shopfront. Workers may update or remove any piece of this information at any time from their profile settings.

What We Never Collect — From Anyone

  • Real-time GPS or device location
  • Device contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Payment or financial information
  • Government ID or any identity document
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data

3. How We Use This Information

Information provided is used solely to:

  • Display worker profiles to customers browsing the app
  • Allow customers to contact workers directly
  • Allow admins to manage categories and city listings

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers — under any circumstances.

4. Firebase & Data Storage

This app uses Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Storage) to store data securely on Google's servers, protected by Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure. For details on how Google manages their servers, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

  • Customer accounts: We retain only an anonymous username and an encrypted password for as long as the account is active. No personal information is ever collected, so there is no personal data to retain.
  • Worker accounts: Professional listing information is retained for as long as the account is active and the worker chooses to keep their listing visible. Individual fields can be edited or removed by the worker at any time.
  • Deleted accounts: All account data is permanently removed from our Firebase database within 10 days of a deletion request.

6. Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data instantly, at any time — no waiting, no approval needed.

To delete your data, simply enter 000 in all profile fields within the app. This immediately overwrites and replaces stored information in the database in real time.

Alternatively, send a deletion request by email to snaptransteam@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your registered username (which may be a nickname — that is all we have on record).

All email-based deletion requests are confirmed within 10 days. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.

7. Location

The app does not access or track real-time GPS location at any point. City selection is performed manually by the user from a dropdown list.

8. Children's Privacy

This is a professional services app built for skilled workers and the people who need them. There is no feature, no flow, and no reason for a child to use it — and because customer accounts require nothing more than a made-up nickname and a password, there is no personal data that a child could even submit.

This app is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. If a child is reported to have created an account — via an in-app report or by contacting us at snaptransteam@gmail.com — that account will be deleted immediately.

9. Security

We use Firebase's built-in encryption and Google's server-side security infrastructure. We have eliminated all unnecessary data collection specifically so that, even in a worst-case scenario, there is nothing sensitive to expose for customers. Workers share only the same professional contact information they already display publicly.
